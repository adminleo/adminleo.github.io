About

Background

I'm a project and innovation leader with over a decade of management and leadership experience. I've founded and scaled companies, led cross-functional teams of up to 100 people across three countries, and owned budgets, partners and funding — steering digital and AI projects from idea to delivery. My specialty is AI you can actually trust and deploy in regulated settings. Most AI guesses the most likely answer; the systems I build reason over structured knowledge — knowledge graphs and ontologies — so every answer stays traceable and defensible under the EU AI Act. In applied research I've explored ontologies for the economy and the public sector. I don't just talk about AI — I build it, and I can explain it to a board and an engineering team alike. I work best where product, technology and people meet — turning complex ideas into clear plans and getting them delivered, whether that's an innovation project, a go-to-market, or an AI initiative.
